## Q&A

### Question: How does ActiveUMI approach the gripper width control and tracking?

ActiveUMI handles gripper width control, actuation, and tracking through a hardware-software combination designed to bridge human teleoperation with robot kinematics:

#### 1. Gripper Open-Close Actuation & Control

- **Micro-Motor Integration**: A micro-motor is mounted directly onto a modified commercial VR controller (Meta Quest 3s) to actuate the open-close motion of the gripper, enabling the operator to control grasping naturally.
- **Non-Invasive Copy**: Rather than replacing or modifying the stock robot gripper, ActiveUMI attaches an identical replica of the robot’s gripper directly to the operator's controller.
- **Action Representation**: The policy records gripper commands as **1-DoF control per arm** (for open/close width) alongside 6-DoF end-effector poses for each arm.

#### 2. 6-DoF Tip Tracking

- **Inside-Out VR Tracking**: The system tracks 6-DoF poses at the **tips of the controllers** (which map 1-to-1 to the real robot's gripper tips) using the Meta Quest headset's inside-out camera system, which triangulates integrated infrared (IR) LEDs in real time.
- **Unified Reference Frame**: Controller tip poses and headset poses are recorded in absolute coordinates relative to a unified world coordinate system established during setup.

#### 3. Precision Calibration & Tracking Accuracy

To ensure accurate execution and tracking alignment, ActiveUMI incorporates three calibration mechanisms:

- **In-Situ Zero-Point Reset**: Operators can reset the 6-DoF coordinate system anytime by pressing the 'B' button on the controller.
- **Gripper Docking Placeholder**: A physical jig acts as a docking station that fixes the controllers at a predefined relative distance and pose for instant frame alignment.
- **Haptic Vibration Feedback**: The controller motor provides high-frequency vibration when a gripper comes within 3 cm of the coordinate origin, giving tactile cues for zero-point alignment.

Through these methods, ActiveUMI reduces the **Relative Pose Error (RPE) to 4.0 mm** (compared to 10.1 mm in traditional UMI), delivering higher trajectory accuracy for policy learning.
