## Question

Which body does the operator hold during handheld data collection on the UR10e?

- **A. Real Dex1-1** as the handheld, motor driven by the PICO controller trigger over RS-485.
- **B. 3D-printed passive copy** of the Dex1-1 (the current [[D01 Handheld gripper body]] decision, accepted 2026-10-19 with the proposal).

Supervisor's aim (meeting of 2026-11-03, [[2026-11-03]]): the handheld exists for simplicity, so it should add as little extra hardware as possible.

## Status as of 2026-11-04 (Martyn)

1. The Dex1-1 has not arrived; the delivery date is unknown (supervisor, 2026-11-03). The RS-485 check on hardware is therefore blocked.
2. One pair = two grippers; two grippers are ordered.
3. The hardware listed for path A (supply, RS-485 adapter, cables, bracket) is the bare minimum for that path.
4. Trigger behaviour: test both proportional and binary, then choose.
5. The printed copy is the test rig until delivery, or the full fallback.
6. Both paths are worked on in parallel until the decision: the copy CAD continues, and the real-path attachment (gripper to PICO controller) is designed next.

## A. Real Dex1-1 as handheld

**For**
- Exact match with the robot gripper: same fingers, camera position and kinematics ([[D01 Handheld gripper body]]).
- Width comes from the motor position `q`; command and achieved width are both recorded, the same signal as VR teleop. No ArUco markers or encoder ([[D05 Gripper width sensing]]; technical note).
- Removes the passive-vs-commanded width mismatch (research gaps B4).
- No mechanism to design or buy: no rails, pinion, lever or spring; the index finger works the controller trigger directly ([[D10 Handheld mechanism]]; trigger readout note).
- Camera stays on the gripper ([[D02 Policy camera]]).
- With two grippers, one can stay on the UR10e and one in the handheld, so the robot stays available and nothing is swapped. Inferred from point 2; whether both grippers ship with the camera (Flagship/Advanced version) is unconfirmed.
- Software path exists: Unitree's `dex1_1_service` exposes DDS topics `rt/dex1/<side>/cmd|state`, 500 Hz loop, x86-64 libraries shipped, so the recording PC can run it (technical note; inferred from source, not run).

**Against / risks**
- Nothing tested on hardware; delivery date unknown, so the check may come late against the end-of-December hardware freeze (`msc-thesis/docs/thesis-plan.md` §5).
- Extra hardware on a tethered handheld: 24–64 V DC supply (24/48 V recommended), 3.5 A peak, no supply in the box ([Unitree](https://unitree.com/Dex1-1); reseller list); RS-485-to-USB adapter (in the box per a [reseller](https://www.usrobotstore.com/products/dex1-1-gripper-compatible-with-all-unitree-g1-models-and-h2), unverified); power and USB cable to the handheld. Whether this still counts as "minimal hardware" is the supervisor's call.
- Mass: 550 g gripper ([Unitree](https://unitree.com/Dex1-1)) plus controller and sleeve (unmeasured). For comparison, UMI 780 g, TacUMI 540 g (research gaps B11).
- Trigger-to-finger latency unmeasured; Unitree's teleop limits the command to 0.18 rad per cycle (technical note).
- No F/T sensor, only `tau_est`; grip force set indirectly through `kp` (technical note).
- Unknown whether the fingers back-drive when unpowered (technical note, Open).
- Position scale disagrees between sources (82 mm, 89 mm, 90 mm); the command-to-width curve must be measured (technical note; D09 row).

**Needs (bare minimum, point 3)**
- 24 or 48 V DC supply, ≥ 3.5 A peak.
- RS-485-to-USB adapter and the XT30PW(2+2) power + data cable (technical note, Wiring).
- Recording PC running `dex1_1_service` and a trigger-to-command program.
- New part: bracket from the gripper's back to the PICO grip sleeve, with its own controller position (Fusion "ASM real-path").

## B. Printed copy as handheld

**For**
- No motor power, supply or data cable on the handheld; light and cheap ([[D01 Handheld gripper body]]).
- Can be built and tested now, before delivery: CAD rebuilt from the official STEP on 2026-11-03 ([[2026-11-03]]).
- Prior work uses printed copies: UMI, RDT2 ([[D01 Handheld gripper body]], Evidence).
- Mechanism copies the Dex1-1 (rack and pinion, MGN9) ([[D10 Handheld mechanism]]).

**Against / risks**
- Width mismatch: the passive handheld records the width where the fingers stop at the object, the Dex1-1 executes a position command; a mapping is needed (research gaps B4).
- Width sensing still to solve: ArUco markers (visibility unchecked) or AS5600 encoder ([[D05 Gripper width sensing]]; research gaps B6).
- Operator holds the squeeze force for the whole grasp (spring return); force not measured (research gaps B5).
- Printed fingers grip differently from the alloy jaws ([[D01 Handheld gripper body]]).
- Camera moved between robot and handheld per session if only one camera is available; remount repeatability unmeasured ([[D02 Policy camera]]; research gaps B8).
- Mechanism may wobble or jam (thesis plan §6 risk); print tests pending (`msc-thesis/hardware/PRINT-TEST-QUEUE.md`).

**Needs**
- 2 MGN9C blocks + 2 MGN9 rails (75 mm), purchase on hold until D01 ([[D10 Handheld mechanism]]).
- Printed fingers, mechanism, lever, sleeve; spring.
- Width sensing (ArUco or AS5600) and the width mapping (B4).

## Shared by both paths

- PICO 4 Ultra Enterprise controller as grip and pose tracker; Vive Tracker 3.0 as fallback ([[D04 Pose tracking]]).
- RGB only, D405 not mounted ([[D03 Depth sensing]]).
- Grip sleeve around the controller, leaving rim and pommel LEDs uncovered ([[D10 Handheld mechanism]]); LED occlusion by the gripper in a gun hold is unmeasured (research gaps B1).
- Controller-to-TCP calibration needs a fixture or a robot-side interface (research gaps B3).

## Fallbacks

- A fails (RS-485 check fails, too heavy, or arrives too late) → B, already built as the test rig (point 5).
- B too inaccurate → swap a real gripper into the handheld (`msc-thesis/docs/thesis-plan.md` §6).
- Dex1-1 delivery delayed → build the copy from the official STEP; build the recorder with any USB camera (thesis plan §6).
- PICO tracking too inaccurate → Vive Tracker 3.0 + base stations ([[D04 Pose tracking]]).
