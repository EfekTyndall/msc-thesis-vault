M3 × 25 bites 6 mm and stops 2.5 mm short, no nut. Clamp ring: 11.0 mm of Ø3.4 holes + nut (2.4 mm), so M3 × 16 leaves about 2.6 mm past the nut. Whether the real gripper's Ø2.5 holes are threaded is unverified (the STEP shows no threads).
