### [[D01 Handheld gripper body]]

Yanmei gave me an insight, that the idea of considering the UMI style workflow is because of simplicity, like collecting data without the need of teleoperating the real robot directly or combination of both workflow. So it is important to design an approach that minimize the addition of extra hardware needed, because this will contradicts with the idea.

The approach of using the real Dex1-1 gripper for both handheld and comes to mind. The consideration comes with the advantage and disadvatages that it gives.

#### Advantages:
- Real width data of the gripper (No encoders or markers needed)
- Motor actuated gripper that could be triggered by the Pico controller 
- Using the high-speed RS-485 serial communication architecture.

#### Disadvantages:
- Currently the information on how we use this RS-485 is not ye researched
- Robot unavailable during collection which is not important
- The focus shift to designing and attachment/adapter so that the Pico controller can be attached.

A 3D-printed copy of the Dex1-1 gripper still valuable because it doesn't cost anything. It easily can be printed on the company 3D printer and we can do some initial test while the real Dex1-1 gripper is being ordered.
