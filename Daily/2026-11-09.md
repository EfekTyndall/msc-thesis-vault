## Measured / learned
- XRoboToolkit PC Service and SDK have no macOS build (Windows, Ubuntu 22.04 x86_64/arm64 only, per the repo READMEs), so the headset stream needs the Linux PC; the Mac only shows the sim.
- Headset kept "connecting" until ufw rules were added on fractal-pc (local subnet only) for TCP 63901 and UDP 42952, the ports the service listened on. Whether both rules were added, and which one is required, is unverified.
- The trigger stays 0.000 until the headset app has **Tracking → Controller** and **Data & Control → Send** switched on ([XRoboToolkit Unity client README](https://github.com/XR-Robotics/XRoboToolkit-Unity-Client)).
- The PC Service does not start on boot: `cd /opt/apps/roboticsservice && nohup setsid bash runService.sh > /tmp/xrsvc.log &`.
