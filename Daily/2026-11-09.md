## Measured / learned
- XRoboToolkit PC Service and SDK have no macOS build (Windows, Ubuntu 22.04 x86_64/arm64 only, per the repo READMEs), so the headset stream needs the Linux PC; the Mac only shows the sim.
- Headset kept "connecting" until ufw rules were added on fractal-pc (local subnet only) for TCP 63901 and UDP 42952.
- Measured with `ss` while streaming: the headset (10.36.1.128) sends about 63 kB/s to the PC Service over **TCP 63901**. The service passes it to the SDK over gRPC on 127.0.0.1:60061, at about 67 kB/s. UDP 42952 received nothing, so its ufw rule is probably unnecessary (not tested). Each headset reconnect left its old TCP connection open, so the guide now restarts the service at session start and stops it at the end.
- The trigger stays 0.000 until the headset app has **Tracking → Controller** and **Data & Control → Send** switched on ([XRoboToolkit Unity client README](https://github.com/XR-Robotics/XRoboToolkit-Unity-Client)).
- The PC Service does not start on boot: `cd /opt/apps/roboticsservice && nohup setsid bash runService.sh > /tmp/xrsvc.log &`.
